The market is asking house builders to work harder for every reservation this spring. On 1 June, Savills cut its 2026 forecast for mainstream UK house prices from a 2% rise to a 2% fall, citing higher mortgage rates and weaker demand. Nationwide recorded the first monthly price fall of the year in May.
