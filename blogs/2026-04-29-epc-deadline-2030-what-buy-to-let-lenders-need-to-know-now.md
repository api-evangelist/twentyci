EPCs have shifted from being a useful reference document to a key regulatory mechanism. In April 2018, Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards (MEES) were introduced, outlining that any rented property would require a minimum EPC rating of E. In 2020, the Government consulted on raising this minimum to C.
